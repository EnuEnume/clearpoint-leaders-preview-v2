4. Cookies and analytics

This page shows a cookie banner before anything non-essential loads. If you choose "Necessary only", no such cookie is set and no data is shared with Meta.

You can change your choice at any time by clearing your browser's site data for this page, or by writing to us.

Some processing may take place outside the United Kingdom; in such cases the safeguards required by UK GDPR apply (adequacy / standard contractual clauses).
